How it works

Video not loading? Watch it on YouTube.

Open your model (STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY or OFF). If it was saved lying down you can stand it up first. Set the size you want in mm, inches or feet, and pick your printers. You can pick more than one, even with different bed sizes.

Hit cut and look over the pieces in the exploded view. If it looks right, generate the plates and open them in Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer. Pick your filament and settings like you normally would, then slice and print.

You can also hollow pieces to save filament, and tiny slivers get merged into the piece next to them so you're not printing a bunch of little scraps.

Questions people have asked

Which slicers does it work with?

Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer, plus slicers built on Orca like QIDI Studio and ElegooSlicer. PrusaSlicer can open the pieces too. Cura can import the pieces but won't get the plate layout.

How big can I go?

There isn't a set limit. It just makes more pieces. It shows you the finished size in every direction before cutting so you can double check it.

Can I use two printers with different bed sizes?

Yes. It plans the cuts around the bigger printer and sends each piece to the smallest printer it fits on. That's how the hand got split between my P1P and A1.

Does one license cover Windows and Mac?

Yes. You get both downloads and can activate it on two computers. The Mac version needs an Apple silicon Mac.

What if my model is broken?

It fixes small problems on its own. If a model is too damaged it'll tell you, and you can repair it in Bambu Studio first (right-click the model, Fix model).
