What it does

The tedious part, done for you.

Cutting a big model by hand means dozens of cuts, connectors and plates. Scale & Cut 3D does all of it in one go.

01

Any size

Set the finished height, width or depth in mm, inches or feet. Stand the model upright first if it was saved lying down.

02

Pieces that fit your printer

Pick your printers, including different bed sizes. Every piece fits, and tiny slivers are merged into their neighbours.

03

Pegs and part numbers

Alignment pegs on every seam, and each cut face shows the number of the piece that goes there. Optional hollowing saves filament.

04

Plates ready to print

Pieces are laid flat and arranged on plates in build order, saved as Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer projects.

Questions

Before you buy.

What does Scale & Cut 3D do?

It scales oversized 3D models, divides them into pieces that fit selected printer beds, optionally adds alignment connectors, and exports slicer-ready projects.

Which slicers are supported?

Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, and Orca-based slicers such as QIDI Studio and ElegooSlicer open the generated projects with every plate laid out. Choose printer, material and process profiles for your machine before slicing. PrusaSlicer and Cura can import the pieces but not the automatic plate layout. Very old versions of Orca-based slicers may need updating to open projects from newer releases.

How big can I print?

There's no fixed limit: the app cuts the model into as many pieces as it needs. Before cutting it shows the finished size in every direction, so you can check it's what you meant.

Can I use printers with different bed sizes for one model?

Yes. Select multiple printer models or add a custom bed size. Scale & Cut 3D plans the cuts around the largest selected build volume, then routes each resulting piece to the smallest selected printer it fits. Large-only pieces remain assigned to the larger bed. Current routing prioritizes fit and does not yet balance equal workloads across every printer.

Which model formats can it open?

STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, and OFF files are supported.

Does one license cover Windows and Mac?

Yes. One purchase includes both downloads and activation on up to two computers. The Mac build currently requires Apple silicon.
